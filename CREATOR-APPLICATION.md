## How would you naturally introduce FLOP to your audience or network?

Through reproducible engineering tutorials and small tools that make FLOP's technical
ideas easier to evaluate. Our project uses 42 logical agent roles under one operator
to organize protocol testing, documentation, and inference-readiness work. We do not
present those roles as independent users or promise airdrop eligibility.

Our first contribution package includes a regression-tested TCLK decoder patch,
reproducible findings in our own offline inference queue, and an offline receipt
verifier. We would explain what each test proves, what remains unverified, and how
another builder can reproduce it without exposing keys or spending tokens. Future
content would distinguish draft specifications from live capabilities and measure
useful outcomes instead of manufacturing activity.
